Sound & inner wellness

Let the bowl speak first

Singing bowls, a steel tongue drum and slow breath. Sound healing helps your body settle so your mind can listen. Tap the bowl and stay with the sound until it fades.

  • Calms a racing mind before a reading
  • Helps release tension held in the body
  • Creates a quiet space to hear yourself
